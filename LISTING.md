# App Store listing copy

Draft copy for the Shopify App Store listing. Character limits are not verified
here — check each field in the Partner Dashboard and trim if it complains.

## Positioning

Not "AI customer support for Shopify". That competes with Gorgias, Tidio, and
Shopify Inbox, which is free and already installed.

The wedge is **Indian D2C stores**: WhatsApp is where their support actually
happens, and their customers write in Hindi and Hinglish. Nobody serious is
aiming at that. Better to be the obvious choice for a small market than the
fifth choice in a large one.

---

## Tagline

> WhatsApp & Hindi AI support that answers for you

Alternatives:

> AI support in Hindi, Hinglish & English, with WhatsApp
> Answer customer questions in any language, 24/7

---

## Introduction

> Zappy answers your customers' questions in Hindi, Hinglish, and English —
> tracks orders, handles returns, and hands off to WhatsApp when a human is
> needed.

---

## Details

> Your customers ask the same questions every day: where is my order, can I
> return this, do you deliver to my pin code. Zappy answers them from your own
> policies, in whatever language the customer writes in.
>
> It only ever answers from what you have saved. It does not invent a return
> window or a delivery date — anything it cannot answer is flagged for you,
> with a drafted reply you can edit and save so it handles that question next
> time.
>
> Customers can track an order, request a return with photos of the damage, ask
> to cancel, or reorder what they bought last time — all inside the chat. When
> they need a person, one tap moves the conversation to WhatsApp.
>
> Free plan available. No code needed — enable the app embed and the chat
> appears on your storefront.

---

## Key benefits

**Answers in your customer's language**
Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi and more. Your knowledge base can stay
in English — Zappy translates the answer.

**WhatsApp handoff built in**
When the assistant can't help, customers continue on WhatsApp in one tap.

**Order tracking, returns, and cancellations**
Customers self-serve inside the chat. Return requests reach you with photos and
a description of the damage.

## What this copy will not fix

A better listing raises conversion for merchants who already found the app. It
does not create the first visitors, and search ranks partly on installs and
reviews — which is exactly what a new app lacks.

The first five installs have to be found by hand: Reddit, Facebook groups for
Shopify sellers, and direct messages to small D2C brands offering to set it up
for them. Ask for a review once the app has actually saved them time, not on
the day they install.
